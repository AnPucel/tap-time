A run is a 3-2-1 countdown, then one full lead-in bar of clicks (watch the grey line sweep across it), then the bar plays 4 times. Turn counting off once you know it, and sing the syllables while you tap. Headphones keep the click out of your mic. “Random bars” builds a fresh bar around the skill you pick, so you can’t just memorise it.
